# Succubus with a Gun

A 2D sidescrolling "run 'n' gun" game inspired both by the classics and the new stuff. Whether you're more familiar with mushrooms and super-vehicles or strawberries and pizzas, this game might be of interest to you.

Follow the interloping demon Samirah as she blasts through a strange dual-sided hell, knocking down the Archdemons of Sin and the ruler of Fireside to bring Iceside back into a balanced playfield.
